01 — Where The Listings Come From

Three supply strategies, layered

The experiences market is massively fragmented — millions of small operators, no single database. Here's how the winners solved it, and how you should for Mumbai.

Approach How It Works Pros Cons
1. Direct Onboarding
(How Klook & Fever started)		 Cold-call local operators, sign them up, digitize their inventory. Klook literally knocked on doors of small operators across Asia who'd never heard of an OTA. Fever hires local city teams. Best prices (no middleman), exclusive inventory, direct control over quality, instant confirmation via your system Extremely labor-intensive. Klook's founders spent months cold-calling. Requires feet on the street in Mumbai.
2. Aggregator APIs
(Fastest launch path)		 Plug into Viator Partner API (300K+ experiences), GetYourGuide API, Bridgify (1M+ experiences), or Holibob. One integration = thousands of listings instantly. Live in weeks, not months. Massive catalog instantly. Proven booking infrastructure. Commission layered (15-30% to platform + your cut). You're reselling, not differentiating. Mumbai-specific coverage may be thin.
3. Hybrid Model
(Recommended for you)		 API aggregation for baseline catalog + direct partnerships with Mumbai's best operators for exclusive, curated inventory. Plus your own "Originals" (Fever model). Best of both worlds. API gives breadth, direct gives depth and exclusivity. Originals give margin and brand. More complex to build and manage. Need both tech and BD teams.

The Klook lesson: "We were literally knocking on their doors cold, hoping to form partnerships." Klook integrated directly with suppliers rather than relying on middlemen — this meant better pricing, instant confirmation, and a seamless UX. They started operator by operator, city by city.

Mumbai's actual supply landscape

Khaki Tours, Reality Tours & Travel, Beyond Heritage, Mumbai Dream Tours, Grand Mumbai Tours, Yo Tours, No Footprints. Most take bookings via WhatsApp or basic websites — ripe for digitization.

Aggregate via hotel APIs (Booking.com Affiliate, OTAs) for broad coverage. Direct deals with boutique stays, heritage hotels, and hostels for exclusive pricing and bundles.

E

Adventure & Outdoor

Alibaug boat operators, Elephanta ferry services, Sanjay Gandhi NP trek guides, cycling groups, kayak operators at Mandwa Beach. Currently book via phone calls.

F

Your Originals

Create your own experiences (like Fever's Candlelight Concerts). Curate "Mumbai After Dark" rooftop crawls, monsoon photography walks, Bollywood dance workshops. Highest margin, strongest brand.

📋 Practical onboarding playbook

  • Week 1-4: Build a simple supplier portal (name, description, photos, pricing, calendar). Make it WhatsApp-friendly — many Mumbai operators run their business from their phone.
  • Week 2-6: On-the-ground BD team of 2-3 people visits operators in Colaba, Fort, Bandra, Juhu. Pitch: "We bring you customers, you just confirm bookings. Zero upfront cost."
  • Week 4-8: Integrate Viator Partner API and/or Bridgify for instant catalog padding. Filter for Mumbai-relevant listings.
  • Week 6-10: Launch 3-5 "Original" curated experiences you design and operate with local guides. These become your hero marketing content.
  • Ongoing: Commission model: 15-20% per booking. Offer operators a free dashboard with analytics, booking management, and review responses.

🔌 Key APIs to integrate

  • Viator Partner API — 300K+ experiences, full booking capability, 8-30% affiliate commission. Best global tour coverage.
  • Bridgify — 1M+ experiences via single API. White-label marketplace option. Built for embedding into platforms.
  • Booking.com Affiliate — Hotel inventory. 25-40% commission on stays booked through your platform.
  • Razorpay — UPI, cards, wallets in one integration. The default for India payments.
  • Google Maps Platform — Places, directions, location-based search for "near me" queries.

Channel 1

SEO & Content — Your #1 Long-Term Growth Engine

SEO is the lowest-cost acquisition channel for travel startups. Target high-intent keywords that travelers actually search. Build programmatic landing pages for every combination: "things to do in Mumbai this weekend," "heritage walks in Colaba," "concerts in Mumbai August 2026," "hotels near Gateway of India." Create editorial guides ("Mumbai Monsoon Survival Guide," "48 Hours in Mumbai") that rank and convert. Travel startups that invest in SEO early achieve 3:1 to 8:1 LTV-to-CAC ratios.

Channel 2

YouTube & Instagram Reels — Creator-Led Discovery

Travel creators on YouTube and Instagram are now more influential than agencies for destination discovery. A single well-produced video can drive more bookings in a month than years of traditional marketing. Klook's "Kreator" program onboarded 20,000+ influencers who earn commissions on curated content — cutting customer acquisition cost by ~25%. Partner with Mumbai food vloggers, heritage walk creators, nightlife accounts. Give them affiliate links + exclusive discount codes.

Channel 3

WhatsApp — India's Distribution Superpower

WhatsApp isn't just messaging in India, it's commerce. Build a WhatsApp Business channel for: booking confirmations, "this weekend" event broadcasts, group travel coordination, customer support. Many Mumbai tour operators already run their business on WhatsApp — meet suppliers and customers where they already are. Allow sharing itineraries and deals via WhatsApp links. This is your viral loop.

Channel 4

Strategic Partnerships & Offline

Partner with hotels to be their recommended "things to do" platform (lobby QR codes, concierge referrals). Integrate with travel apps like MakeMyTrip, Ixigo as an experiences layer. Corporate tie-ups for team outings and offsite bookings. Airport/railway station signage for arriving tourists. Tie-ups with co-working spaces for "weekend plans" curation boards.

🎯 The Fever Playbook (adapt for Mumbai)

Fever's city launch strategy, proven across 450+ cities:

  • Step 1 — Audience first: Build a local digital media audience BEFORE launching the marketplace. Start an Instagram/YouTube channel about "things to do in Mumbai" with genuine editorial value.
  • Step 2 — "Secret Media Network": Fever mines search trends and social signals to predict which experiences will succeed before investing in them. Do the same — monitor what Mumbaikars are searching for, talking about, sharing.
  • Step 3 — Launch with Originals: Don't just aggregate — create 3-5 signature experiences that only exist on your platform. This gives press something to write about and users a reason to choose you over Klook.
  • Step 4 — 4-week city launch sprint: Fever compresses new city launches to 4 weeks using a standardized tech stack and playbook. Curate 30-50 launch experiences, onboard 10-15 key operators, run a launch event.

💰 Unit Economics Targets

Based on industry benchmarks from Klook, Headout, and travel startup data:

  • Commission rate: 15-25% per booking (activities), 10-15% (hotels)
  • Target CAC: ₹150-300 per first-time buyer via SEO/content, ₹500-800 via paid ads
  • Target LTV-to-CAC ratio: 3:1 minimum, 8:1 for top performers
  • Average order value: ₹1,200-2,500 (activities), ₹3,500-8,000 (hotel + activity bundles)
  • Repeat rate target: 30-40% within 6 months (Headout reports strong repeat from urban locals)

📊 Top Gen Z Travel Trends India 2026

  • Short getaways over long vacations — Weekend trips from Mumbai (Lonavala, Alibaug, Mahabaleshwar, Goa) are the sweet spot
  • Experience over luxury — A ₹300 street meal matters more than ₹3,000 fine dining. Authenticity wins.
  • Spiritual tourism exploding — 53% of spiritual travel bookings are Gen Z. Routes like Pune-Nanded, Delhi-Rishikesh trending hard.
  • Music & event-led travel — Concerts, festivals, and cultural events are primary trip triggers, not afterthoughts.
  • Spontaneous planning — Book on-trip, not weeks ahead. 70%+ of Klook bookings happen while already at the destination.
  • Instagram-first decisions — Destinations chosen for "that one perfect window shot." Cafés picked for aesthetics, not just food.
  • "Book now, pay later" — 35% of Gen Z say installment plans decide how many trips they take.
  • Hidden gems over hotspots — 69% of Gen Z prefer places off their feeds. #hiddengems posts rose 50%+ on TikTok.
  • Creator-led discovery — YouTube and Instagram creators drive more bookings than traditional agencies.
  • Slow travel — Lingering in one place rather than city-hopping. Pondicherry-style minimalism trending.

🌏 International — Gen Z from India

For international travel, Gen Z Indians are drawn to: Japan (#1 globally for Gen Z per Airbnb), Thailand, Vietnam, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bali. Budget-consciousness is key — daily backpacker budgets, hostel stays, local transport. Phu Quoc (Vietnam) was the breakout search destination of 2025. However, 60% prefer domestic, meaning the domestic opportunity is 1.5x larger than international.

Your positioning insight: Gen Z doesn't want a "vacation" — they want a personality expression. They travel for content, for spiritual exploration, for food, for "the vibe." Your platform should position experiences as identity, not itinerary. "What kind of Mumbaikar are you?" not "Book tours in Mumbai."

Build templated pages at scale for every searchable combination:

  • /things-to-do-in-mumbai-{when} → "this-weekend," "today," "in-monsoon," "in-december"

Each page auto-populates with relevant listings, reviews, pricing, and booking CTAs. This is how Headout and Klook dominate organic search — thousands of long-tail pages that each capture specific traveler intent.

05 — The Launch Playbook

90-day Mumbai launch plan

Adapted from Fever's 4-week city launch, Klook's operator-first approach, and Headout's mobile-first strategy.

Weeks 1-3

Foundation — Supply & Tech

Build MVP: listing pages, search, booking flow, payment (Razorpay). Integrate one aggregator API (Viator or Bridgify) for instant catalog. BD team starts visiting top 20 Mumbai operators (Khaki Tours, Reality Tours, food tour operators, NMACC). Goal: 50+ bookable experiences on day one.

Weeks 4-6

Audience Building — Before Launch

Launch Instagram account with "Mumbai insider" content — hidden spots, street food reels, heritage stories. Publish 10 SEO blog posts targeting highest-volume keywords. Build WhatsApp broadcast list. Identify and partner with 5-10 Mumbai micro-influencers (food, travel, lifestyle). Create 3 "Original" experiences exclusive to your platform.

Weeks 7-9

Launch — Go Live

Launch event at an iconic Mumbai venue (think Gateway of India sunset event, or a rooftop in Bandra). First 500 users get ₹500 credit. Press outreach to Mumbai Mirror, LBB Mumbai, Timeout Mumbai, travel YouTubers. Launch referral program: "Share with a friend, both get ₹200 off." Run targeted Instagram/Google ads for top 5 keywords. Goal: 1,000 bookings in first month.

Weeks 10-13

Iterate & Scale

Analyze booking data: which categories convert, which neighborhoods are hot, where users drop off. Double down on winning categories. Expand operator roster to 100+. Launch hotel vertical with bundle pricing. Build loyalty program (Klook-style points). Begin planning expansion to Pune/Goa as nearby markets. Set up review system to build social proof flywheel.

The unfair advantage: Global platforms like Klook and Headout have thin Mumbai coverage. BookMyShow only does events. Airbnb Experiences has limited Mumbai supply. LBB curates but doesn't book. There's a clear whitespace for a platform that combines hotels + activities + events + food + heritage with deep Mumbai-native curation and booking. Own Mumbai first, then expand.
