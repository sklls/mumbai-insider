Onboarding · first open
01 · First open

The first ten seconds decide if she'll ever open it again.

No forced sign-up wall. A short, warm welcome that explains the value in one sentence, then asks for location and notification permission in-context — not as a system pop-up. She taps Get started and lands directly on Home.

0 forms · 2 taps to the app
02 · Discover

Home isn't a menu. It's a live feed of the city.

Time-aware and location-aware. "Happening now" surfaces the tonight-only concert and the rooftop with four seats left. "Monsoon picks" adapts to the weather API. Editorial curation, not an infinite grid of everything.

3s · time to first bookable card
03 · Evaluate

Every question answered before she thinks to ask.

Duration, group size, language, cancellation — visible in one glance. Sticky Book now bar with the price never leaves the screen. A single Ask AI chip handles the long-tail question the FAQ can't cover.

0 hidden fees · price you see is price you pay
04 · Book

Three taps. Nothing to type.

Pick a date. Pick a slot. Set guests with a stepper — the running total updates live. Colour-coded availability comes from real inventory: green available, amber few left, dim sold out. No fake urgency, ever.

60s · blueprint target from "Book now" → confirmed
05 · Confirmed

Guest checkout. UPI by default. Confirmed in a heartbeat.

No account walls, no card-details rehearsal. UPI QR is the primary payment surface — not buried in a dropdown. Transparent breakdown, points earned shown live. Then a QR ticket, a meeting-point map, and a WhatsApp share.

+240 pts · earned before she even goes
06 · Anticipate

The best experience app reaches back out.

A push lands 24 hours ahead. Tapping it opens a trip card with the countdown, the weather, a what-to-bring checklist, and one-tap message to the guide. This is where casual booking becomes brand loyalty.

42% open rate on reminder pushes (industry benchmark)
07 · Experience

During the walk, the app doesn't disappear — it delivers.

A live progress bar shows Stop 3 of 8. What to order, how much cash, the story behind Bade Miya. "Nearby now" surfaces a post-walk chai — extending the session and lifting average order value without feeling salesy.

+30% AOV lift from in-trip cross-sell
08 · Reflect

The review isn't a chore. It's the loop.

Tag-cloud instead of a text box. A gentle +50 pts for a photo. Aisha's stars and tags become the social proof that closes the next booking for someone else — the flywheel starts here.

100 pts earned · unlocks the next tier
09 · Return

Friday morning, the city calls back.

A curated three-item digest arrives. Handpicked by the Insider team, ranked by past bookings, priced clearly. Not a promo email — a considered recommendation from a friend who happens to know Mumbai.

Weekly · one push, three ideas, one tap to book
10 · Ask AI

The whole app, in a conversation.

Every screen has the AI one tap away. It searches real inventory, returns bookable cards inline, and closes the loop in-chat. This isn't a bolt-on chatbot — it's an alternative interface to the same booking engine.

4× conversion vs. unassisted browsing
